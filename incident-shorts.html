incident-shorts

incident-shorts is a personal tool by Kostas that publishes short videos to his own YouTube channel. Each video explains one real tech incident (an outage, a breach or an engineering failure) in under a minute.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished videos to the owner's channel as private videos. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
